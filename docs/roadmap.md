# Research and development roadmap

Milestones describe delivered capabilities and continuing research, rather than separately published software versions.

## v0.1.0: Archive foundations

**Delivered.** Site foundations, evidence methodology and reference register are available.

Next: Maintain accessibility, source integrity and correction handling.

## v0.2.0: Biblical foundations

**Delivered.** Individual Biblical studies, passage citations and thematic reading collections are available.

Next: Expand contextual analysis and Scripture coverage.

## v0.3.0: Demonic encyclopaedia

**Delivered.** Illustrated entity profiles, search, classifications and original-language terminology are available.

Next: Develop additional profiles with verified individual attestations.

## v0.4.0: Fallen angels and spiritual hierarchies

**In development.** Studies of rebellion, Genesis 6, Watchers and spiritual powers are available.

Next: Deepen comparative readings and Aramaic manuscript research.

## v0.5.0: Historical research archive

**In development.** Manuscript records, historical timeline and critical later-literature studies are available.

Next: Expand documented historical encounters and independent corroboration.

## v0.6.0: Ancient literature and early Church

**In development.** Enochic literature, early Church writings and medieval illuminations are available.

Next: Expand ancient textual witnesses, patristic comparisons and manuscript metadata.

## v0.7.0: Spiritual warfare and deliverance

**Delivered.** Individual studies of spiritual warfare, discernment, prohibited practices and Biblical deliverance are available.

Next: Expand remaining Gospel and Acts accounts and deepen pastoral and historical context.

## v0.8.0: Connection atlas

**Delivered.** Interactive connection atlas with illustrated study cards, shared sources, relationship filters and fullscreen exploration is available.

Next: Refine graph navigation and verify accessibility across screen sizes.

## v0.9.0: Scripture explorer

**Delivered.** Searchable Scripture index grouped by Biblical book, translation links and cross-references are available.

Next: Improve passage normalisation and coverage.

## v1.0.0: Public release

**Planned.** Editorial publication is authorised; the archive remains in development.

Next: Complete release-quality accessibility, link, metadata and deployment verification; public search indexing remains a separate release decision.
