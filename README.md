# Demonology

**A Biblical Study of the Unseen Realm**

*Exploring the Darkness Through the Light of Scripture*

Demonology is a Christian research archive exploring spiritual warfare, fallen angels, demonic entities and supernatural deception through Scripture, historical documents and credible scholarship.

## Our purpose

The archive approaches these subjects from a Biblical, Trinitarian Christian perspective. Jesus Christ's authority over evil is central to its research. Scripture is distinguished from historical testimony, theological interpretation and extra-Biblical tradition.

## Explore the archive

- Biblical foundations and theological research
- Demonic entities, fallen angels and spiritual hierarchies
- Scripture index and reference library
- Historical records, ancient literature and early Church writings
- Spiritual warfare and Christian responses to evil

The collection is under development. Research drafts are clearly identified and do not represent final editorial approval.

Read the [research methodology](docs/research-methodology.md), [roadmap](docs/roadmap.md) and [changelog](CHANGELOG.md) for further information.

## Responsible research

The archive is educational. It does not promote occult practices, encourage diagnoses of possession or replace medical care. Historical claims are attributed to their sources, and uncertainty is acknowledged.

Original material is not offered under an open licence. Third-party materials retain their respective rights.
